I am delighted to share that I am a joint Ph.D. student at **Nanjing University** and the **[Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CASIA)](http://english.ia.cas.cn/)**, affiliated with the **Pattern Recognition Laboratory (PRLab)** and advised by **Prof. Chenyang Si** and **Prof. Liang Wang**. My Ph.D. research will focus on **VLM agents**, **post-training**, and **Embodied AI**, with an emphasis on building agents that can perceive, reason, and act in dynamic environments.
